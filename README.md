# Chaturvedi Money School, season 1

Instagram Reel files for [@chaturvedi_ai_family](https://www.instagram.com/chaturvedi_ai_family/).

`reels/epNN.mp4` and `reels/epNN-cover.jpg` are the 25 episodes and their covers, kept here so Instagram can
fetch them when each one is published. `posting_plan.json` lists every episode with its caption, hashtags and
publish time (India time): episode 1 on 20 Nov 2026, then 1:00 PM and 7:30 PM daily until 2 Dec 2026.

Visuals are AI-generated (Nim, Veo 3.1 Lite and Seedance) and edited with ReelForge.
